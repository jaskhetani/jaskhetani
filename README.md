## Builder lesson: event ticketing under pressure

One of my clearest builder lessons came from a client/event ticketing project: I had to support QR-based entry for hundreds of attendees under low-network, multi-scanner conditions.

The MVP worked, but AppSheet + Google Sheets exposed real limits around sync, concurrency, and duplicate prevention. Splitting flows across VIP, physical, and online passes solved the immediate event problem — and taught me why stronger database design, queueing, and operational constraints matter when software touches real people.

That is the kind of work I enjoy: the uncomfortable space where user expectations, technical limits, and deadline pressure all arrive at once.
